# Vizzy Roadmap
Updated: 2026-10-24 · Owner: Vizzy · North star: pasteable, opinionated charts for writers (link + PNG), chart excellence before My BI.

## Open (ranked; knock off from the top)
1. **Standing live discovery.** Before other product work, run an anonymous pass on vizzy.run, file each failure as an Open item, and verify that path on the live site. Sessions on 2026-10-24 covered about nine charts, and the free tier then blocked more runs. A signed-in pass the same day ran 14 charts as Connor. The page showed Unlimited and then "50 left · generate whenever". That 50 did not change. The account was never blocked, so the unchanged 50 is not a separate failure. Chart failures from that pass are filed in the items below. A signed-in phone pass at 390px the same day ran 9 charts. Failures from that pass are filed in the items below. An anonymous pass on 2026-10-24 ran signed out. It made about three charts, and the free tier blocked a fourth. The solar check passed because the title was "Solar capacity added by country in 2023". The chart still plotted new additions, not total installed capacity. Check: on https://vizzy.run, repeat that prompt, and either the chart is total installed capacity for 2023 or the title says it is new capacity. File whatever still fails as the next Open item.
2. **Keep source labels honest.** A vaccine and autism prompt was reframed, then the chart invented study counts. The source estimate said "No published page backs these numbers." "Wild tigers by country" labeled Wikipedia "official" and put no year in the title. A vague eggs prompt in the signed-in pass drew two estimate points with no backing page. Check: Wikipedia is not labeled official, the tiger title names a year, and the chart does not print counts when the source says no published page backs them.
3. **Show a sparse or empty chart as unfinished.** A vague eggs prompt drew two estimate points with no backing page and presented them as a finished chart. The gun-ownership-by-age chart drew empty bars for 50-64 and 65+ and showed no warning. Check: the eggs chart does not present those two points as a finished series. The gun chart warns that 50-64 and 65+ are empty, or it does not draw those empty bars as values.
4. **Match the date note to the axis.** On 3 of 14 signed-in charts the date or range note did not match the axis. On the 2026-10-24 anonymous pass, "Extreme poverty 1990-2023" drew the x-axis past 2023 through 2026. Ticks included 24 and 26. Context said about 10.5% in 2023. The line ended at 9.8%. On the phone pass, "US federal funds rate since 2020" drew sparse bars from 2000 through 2026. Context said "from 2020 onward". "Antarctic sea ice extent 1979-2023 NSIDC" ran the x-axis to 2026. Check: the range in the note and the labels on the axis are the same span, the axis does not run past the end year in the prompt, and a context percentage for a year matches the line at that year. US federal funds rate since 2020 is a line from 2020 onward. It does not draw sparse bars from 2000 through 2026. Antarctic sea ice for 1979-2023 does not run the axis to 2026.
5. **Ask before a vague prompt.** A vague prompt generates a guess. "eggs" drew egg production. "is the economy doing better?" drew two unitless bars, Retail sales and Industrial production, on a y-axis from 0 to 1.5. `clarifyingQuestion` in `honor-prompt.ts` returns a measure question for an economy better or worse prompt. Compose shows that string in `compose-fail` before `consumeSlot`. Show 1 to 3 question chips on the compose card before `consumeSlot`. A "Just send it" control skips the chips and generates. Check: "eggs" asks about price, production, or nutrition, and shows "Just send it". "is the economy doing better?" asks for the measure and the period, and shows "Just send it". US unemployment since 2019 generates with no interrupt.
6. **Keep labels readable.** Labels were cut at spaces. "United States" became "States", satellite labels became "States" and "Kingdom", and "Burkina Faso" became "Faso". The satellite long tail was unreadable. Fed funds ticks read "00/01". Arctic sea ice mixed year formats on the x-axis. In the signed-in pass, 5 of 14 charts still mangled labels. "New Jersey" became "Jersey". "San Marino" became "Marino". Spaces disappeared in "Q22020", "Jan2025", and "SweaterWeather". Years showed as two digits. On the phone pass, "United States" again rendered as "States". A prompt for every US state showed about 15 states. A Bitcoin label read "35.0408k". Check: "United States", "Burkina Faso", "New Jersey", and "San Marino" render in full, the satellite long tail is readable, fed funds ticks are not "00/01", Arctic sea ice uses one year format, "Q2 2020", "Jan 2025", and "Sweater Weather" keep their spaces, and years are not two digits. A prompt for every US state does not stop at about 15 states. A Bitcoin amount does not render as "35.0408k".
7. **Keep typos out of the title.** On 2 of 14 signed-in charts the chart understood the prompt and the title kept the typos. Check: the title spells the words the chart resolved.
8. **Finish the context panel.** On results read soon after render, including Arctic sea ice and the solar chart, the panel stayed on "Reading the chart…". On the phone pass the panel stayed on "Reading the chart…" for about 30 to 40 seconds before Keep learning appeared. Check: after a chart renders, the panel leaves "Reading the chart…" without a reload, and Keep learning appears without a wait of about 30 seconds.
9. **Point New chart, the address bar, and Library at the chart you just made.** "New chart" cycles a demo prompt and does not clear the composer. A reload was required. Share copies a URL under https://vizzy.run/c/, and the address bar stays on /. On every attempt in the signed-in pass, a bare click on New chart did nothing. A chart was generated only after text was typed into the Revise box. Across all 14 charts the address bar never showed /c/. Share was the only copy of the URL. Library listed none of them. On the phone pass, 9 charts were generated at 390px. Library stayed empty. The address bar stayed on /. New chart generated from the typed text. Revise with a new topic edited the current chart. Check: New chart clears the composer. A bare click on New chart generates a chart without text already in the Revise box. After generate, the address bar is the /c/ URL that Share copies. Library lists the charts from that session. On a 390px page, New chart generates a new chart from the typed text. Revise with a new topic does not keep the previous subject. After 9 charts in that signed-in session, Library lists them and the address bar is the /c/ URL Share copies. A personal library beyond that list stays in Later.
10. **Match the free-chart count to server enforcement before submit.** The counter went from 3 to 2 to 1, then back to 3. A later click hit a paywall while the idle page still said a free chart was available. After cookies were cleared, the homepage showed "3 free today", and POST /api/compose returned HTTP 402 with no chart. On the 2026-10-24 anonymous pass the counter still read "3 free today" after three generated charts. The fourth prompt hit the paywall and produced no chart. Check: when the homepage says "3 free today", POST /api/compose does not return HTTP 402, the idle count matches the server before submit, and three generated charts do not leave the counter at "3 free today".

## Shipped
- **Wrong series still live** — 2026-10-24. A prompt for US, China, and India GDP growth since 2000 draws that growth. It does not draw protected waters, Monaco, or Palau. A prompt for Antarctic sea ice extent 1979-2023 draws the Antarctic series, not Arctic sea ice. Alias matches use a word boundary, and the mpa alias is gone. [#44](https://github.com/bytesofconnor/vizzy/pull/44).
- **Generate from Keep learning** — 2026-10-24. One tap on Keep learning generates the next chart in the chart slot. It does not focus the composer. A second Generate is not required. A monthly series asked for a yearly change mints one point per year. [#43](https://github.com/bytesofconnor/vizzy/pull/43).
- **Plot the dataset the prompt names** — 2026-10-24. A prompt that names a dataset draws that series and that year span, including a series served from cache. When another series is drawn, the title and the note name that series. A note does not say Pew when the source is Gallup. [#39](https://github.com/bytesofconnor/vizzy/pull/39) and [#40](https://github.com/bytesofconnor/vizzy/pull/40). On the phone pass the same day, two prompts that name a dataset still drew a different series. Those checks shipped in [#44](https://github.com/bytesofconnor/vizzy/pull/44).
- **Keep every named series, or say which series dropped** — 2026-10-24. A prompt that names several series keeps each series the lookup table contains, or the title names the series and the range that were drawn. [#37](https://github.com/bytesofconnor/vizzy/pull/37).
- **Match chart type, units, and rank encoding to the prompt** — 2026-10-24. A monthly series such as fed funds is a line. Rank is not bar height. A rate and a count do not share one axis. [#38](https://github.com/bytesofconnor/vizzy/pull/38). On the phone pass, "US federal funds rate since 2020" drew sparse bars. The year span and the line are Open item 4.
- **Honor the prompt, or say what changed** — 2026-10-24. A chart that cannot match the prompt's metric, place, or time grain says so in the title. "Is the economy doing better?" asks which measure and does not title itself Sep 2026. [#36](https://github.com/bytesofconnor/vizzy/pull/36). On that phone pass, "is the economy doing better?" drew two unitless bars. The chip check is Open item 5.
- **Clear generate button on the homepage** — 2026-10-23. Live CTA is "Generate my chart". Already on vizzy.run. [35511fa](https://github.com/bytesofconnor/vizzy/commit/35511fabd79e0a310a5195cecdff2e25698c99d0).
- **Pricing line on the homepage** — 2026-10-23. Live note is "3 free today, then $8 for 25". Pack infra is `FREE_PER_DAY=3`, `PACK_CREDITS=25`, `PACK_CENTS=800`, Stripe and Convex. Already on vizzy.run. Copy in [35511fa](https://github.com/bytesofconnor/vizzy/commit/35511fabd79e0a310a5195cecdff2e25698c99d0).
