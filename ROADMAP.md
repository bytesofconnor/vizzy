# Vizzy Roadmap
Updated: 2026-10-24 · Owner: Vizzy · North star: pasteable, opinionated charts for writers (link + PNG), chart excellence before My BI.

## Open (ranked; knock off from the top)
1. **Standing live discovery.** Before other product work, run an anonymous pass on vizzy.run, file each failure as an Open item, and verify that path on the live site. Sessions on 2026-10-24 covered about nine charts, and the free tier then blocked more runs. A signed-in pass the same day ran 14 charts as Connor. The page showed Unlimited and then "50 left · generate whenever". That 50 did not change. The account was never blocked, so the unchanged 50 is not a separate failure. Chart failures from that pass are filed in the items below. An anonymous pass on 2026-10-24 ran signed out. It made about three charts, and the free tier blocked a fourth. The solar check passed because the title was "Solar capacity added by country in 2023". The chart still plotted new additions, not total installed capacity. Check: on https://vizzy.run, repeat that prompt, and either the chart is total installed capacity for 2023 or the title says it is new capacity. File whatever still fails as the next Open item.
2. **Plot the dataset the prompt names.** On 3 of 14 signed-in charts the title and context described the prompt. The drawn series was a different dataset, or the note named the wrong source. "Extreme poverty 1990-2023" plotted OWID world population, in people, from year -10000 through 2023. "Hurricane deaths by storm 2024" plotted hurricane counts since 1851, and Context claimed deaths. A gun-ownership-by-age chart had empty bars for 50-64 and 65+. The note said Pew. The source showed Gallup. On the 2026-10-24 anonymous pass, "Hurricane deaths by storm 2024" was titled "Direct Deaths by 2024 Atlantic Hurricanes". The bars included tropical storms, among them Alberto, Chris, Sara, and Nadine. Check: the poverty chart is extreme poverty for 1990 to 2023, or the title and context say it is world population. The hurricane chart is deaths, or the title and context say it is storm counts since 1851. A title that says Hurricanes either includes only hurricanes or says tropical storms are included. The gun chart does not say Pew when the source is Gallup.
3. **Keep every named series, or say which series dropped.** On 4 of 14 signed-in charts a multi-series prompt became one series, or an unclear pair, with no note. A renewables chart for Germany, France, and Spain was mostly Germany. Netflix versus Disney+ was an unclear dual series. The range was truncated. Austin, Denver, and Miami rent became Austin 2025 only. US nuclear, coal, and gas became one line. Check: the renewables chart shows Germany, France, and Spain, or the title says it is mostly Germany. Netflix and Disney+ are two series, or the title names the series and the range it drew. The rent chart shows Austin, Denver, and Miami, or the title says Austin 2025. The fuel chart shows nuclear, coal, and gas, or the title names the one series.
4. **Keep source labels honest.** A vaccine and autism prompt was reframed, then the chart invented study counts. The source estimate said "No published page backs these numbers." "Wild tigers by country" labeled Wikipedia "official" and put no year in the title. A vague eggs prompt in the signed-in pass drew two estimate points with no backing page. Check: Wikipedia is not labeled official, the tiger title names a year, and the chart does not print counts when the source says no published page backs them.
5. **Show a sparse or empty chart as unfinished.** A vague eggs prompt drew two estimate points with no backing page and presented them as a finished chart. The gun-ownership-by-age chart drew empty bars for 50-64 and 65+ and showed no warning. Check: the eggs chart does not present those two points as a finished series. The gun chart warns that 50-64 and 65+ are empty, or it does not draw those empty bars as values.
6. **Match the date note to the axis.** On 3 of 14 signed-in charts the date or range note did not match the axis. On the 2026-10-24 anonymous pass, "Extreme poverty 1990-2023" drew the x-axis past 2023 through 2026. Ticks included 24 and 26. Context said about 10.5% in 2023. The line ended at 9.8%. Check: the range in the note and the labels on the axis are the same span, the axis does not run past the end year in the prompt, and a context percentage for a year matches the line at that year.
7. **Keep labels readable.** Labels were cut at spaces. "United States" became "States", satellite labels became "States" and "Kingdom", and "Burkina Faso" became "Faso". The satellite long tail was unreadable. Fed funds ticks read "00/01". Arctic sea ice mixed year formats on the x-axis. In the signed-in pass, 5 of 14 charts still mangled labels. "New Jersey" became "Jersey". "San Marino" became "Marino". Spaces disappeared in "Q22020", "Jan2025", and "SweaterWeather". Years showed as two digits. Check: "United States", "Burkina Faso", "New Jersey", and "San Marino" render in full, the satellite long tail is readable, fed funds ticks are not "00/01", Arctic sea ice uses one year format, "Q2 2020", "Jan 2025", and "Sweater Weather" keep their spaces, and years are not two digits.
8. **Keep typos out of the title.** On 2 of 14 signed-in charts the chart understood the prompt and the title kept the typos. Check: the title spells the words the chart resolved.
9. **Finish the context panel.** On results read soon after render, including Arctic sea ice and the solar chart, the panel stayed on "Reading the chart…". Check: after a chart renders, the panel leaves "Reading the chart…" without a reload.
10. **Point New chart, the address bar, and Library at the chart you just made.** "New chart" cycles a demo prompt and does not clear the composer. A reload was required. Share copies a URL under https://vizzy.run/c/, and the address bar stays on /. On every attempt in the signed-in pass, a bare click on New chart did nothing. A chart was generated only after text was typed into the Revise box. Across all 14 charts the address bar never showed /c/. Share was the only copy of the URL. Library listed none of them. Check: New chart clears the composer. A bare click on New chart generates a chart without text already in the Revise box. After generate, the address bar is the /c/ URL that Share copies. Library lists the charts from that session. A personal library beyond that list stays in Later.
11. **Match the free-chart count to server enforcement before submit.** The counter went from 3 to 2 to 1, then back to 3. A later click hit a paywall while the idle page still said a free chart was available. After cookies were cleared, the homepage showed "3 free today", and POST /api/compose returned HTTP 402 with no chart. On the 2026-10-24 anonymous pass the counter still read "3 free today" after three generated charts. The fourth prompt hit the paywall and produced no chart. Check: when the homepage says "3 free today", POST /api/compose does not return HTTP 402, the idle count matches the server before submit, and three generated charts do not leave the counter at "3 free today".

## Shipped
- **Match chart type, units, and rank encoding to the prompt** — 2026-10-24. A monthly series such as fed funds is a line. Rank is not bar height. A rate and a count do not share one axis. [#38](https://github.com/bytesofconnor/vizzy/pull/38).
- **Honor the prompt, or say what changed** — 2026-10-24. A chart that cannot match the prompt's metric, place, or time grain says so in the title. "Is the economy doing better?" asks which measure and does not title itself Sep 2026. [#36](https://github.com/bytesofconnor/vizzy/pull/36).
- **Clear generate button on the homepage** — 2026-10-23. Live CTA is "Generate my chart". Already on vizzy.run. [35511fa](https://github.com/bytesofconnor/vizzy/commit/35511fabd79e0a310a5195cecdff2e25698c99d0).
- **Pricing line on the homepage** — 2026-10-23. Live note is "3 free today, then $8 for 25". Pack infra is `FREE_PER_DAY=3`, `PACK_CREDITS=25`, `PACK_CENTS=800`, Stripe and Convex. Already on vizzy.run. Copy in [35511fa](https://github.com/bytesofconnor/vizzy/commit/35511fabd79e0a310a5195cecdff2e25698c99d0). Constants in [6e004ec](https://github.com/bytesofconnor/vizzy/commit/6e004ecfa03f5c21676151a55477ae3e8efe7add).
