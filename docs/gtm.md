# GTM

Proof-led distribution into writers. Not launch theater.

Vizzy is a craft tool that earns trust one published chart at a time. For the first 60–90 days, run **one motion only**.

## 1. Fix the door, then invite people in

Ship homepage fixes first: clear generate CTA, “3 free, then $8 for 25,” sharper outcome headline. Do not spend attention on a broken first minute.

## 2. Become the chart in other people’s posts

The best ad is a Vizzy chart sitting inside a real article. Make excellent charts from real public questions (sports, markets, climate, local). Publish them on X with the chart image + link, and wherever you write. Each post should feel useful even if nobody clicks — the chart is the content; Vizzy is the signature.

## 3. Seed the AI path without leading with “AI charts”

A tiny “paste this into ChatGPT / Claude” prompt on the site and in posts. Writers already live in those tools; Vizzy is the artifact layer they call when they need something honest to publish. Site copy: `AgentPaste` / `lib/agent-paste.ts`. Always point models at `https://vizzy.run`.

## 4. Convert on delight, not funnel tricks

Free chart → obvious quality → paywall with a plain pack price. No freemium maze. Measure: charts created, % that export/copy, return in 7 days, pack purchases.

## 5. Delay everything that smells like a startup launch

No Product Hunt, no “Personal BI” campaign, no dashboard screenshots, no ads — until strangers are already embedding charts and a few have paid.

## Why

Early, taste is the product, and writers trust what they can paste. Broad awareness before chart excellence is visible in the wild burns the name into “another AI chart thing.”

Sharper loop until something clearly works: post three times a week with Vizzy charts for 30 days. Draft the posts, pick the chart prompts, tighten the site copy. That is the whole GTM.
